02 / Who I Am

More than a stack of logos.

“I like the moment when two stubborn systems—or a group of friends around a table—finally click.”

I’m Zachary Lloyd Jones—most folks just call me Zack, or spot me online as Weatwagon. My career sits at the intersection of software development and systems integration: taking messy, disconnected tools and turning them into workflows that quietly do their job.

Outside of work, I’m a lifelong tinkerer. If there’s a self-hosted server to spin up, a new AI image model to wire into ComfyUI, or a quality-of-life script that makes game night run smoother, I’ll probably spend a weekend building it just to see how far it can go.
